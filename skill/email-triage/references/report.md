# Report

Write in Chinese with meaningful original names/subjects. Lead with actions, important changes and exceptions; omit empty sections.

Important mail normally needs 2–4 sentences: what it concerns, date/time/place, and required action or deadline. Combine related messages around the latest supported arrangement. Keep source account and subject traceable. Say “原文未提供” for missing fields or “未能读取，待核查” for unreadable content.

Present candidates as concrete events/tasks with dates and any missing fields. The user can confirm them by name or meaning in this same task; retain stable IDs internally without requiring the user to copy them. Do not duplicate candidate content in another summary.

Routine ads, codes and low-value notices need no individual report. Hide normal counts, cursor progress and scheduling details unless requested or needed to explain an exception. Never repeat secrets.

After all pages and state recording succeed, deliver a final report. When there is nothing worth reporting and no exception, use exactly:

本次整理完成，暂无需要关注的新内容。

For a partial failure, state what completed, what remains unread/unprocessed and the next step. Do not send the completion receipt for an uncertain run. Reports stay in the Codex task; do not create local Markdown reports or raw mail archives.
